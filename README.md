A little vibe coded tool for Honkai: Star Rail.

Track your Stellar Jade and Special passes. 
Track a character you're planning to pull and see the high bar (THE WORST CASE SCENERIO; Default = 180 Special Passes), and the low bar (rough average/expected minimum based on your luck stats/pull history).
Track your "claimables" (events, achievements, world-based progression, etc.) and see how much Stellar Jade and/or Special Passes you have left.
Track your pull history and see your odds and win streaks.
